Try a sample QR

How to read it

  • A sample QR starting with OLD is a sample from before the switch: no seal asked.
  • Any other QR is a sealed sample: it asks for its seal unless the seal is already in the tray.
  • A seal number starting with BAD is tampered (red cross); any other number is intact (green tick).
  • Moisture and fineness logins share the LAB seal tray.

Entry

In Preview mode a greyed seal box appears above Register. In On mode, after Register is pressed a window asks for two seals (interchangeable).
